Help

Choose a little time to put your phone aside.

Getting started

Choose how long you want to stay off your phone, tap Start phone-free time, then put your phone aside.

During the timer

Keep the app open; the screen will dim automatically. Switching apps, locking the screen or interrupting the timer marks this challenge unfinished. Your previous records remain.

Ending early

Hold End early continuously for 3 seconds to end this challenge. A countdown appears while you hold; release to cancel.

XP Boost Cards

Where available, claim a card by completing a rewarded ad. No ad click is needed, and earning a card does not start a challenge. Choose to use a card before starting; one card is consumed when the challenge is saved. Success earns 1.5 times normal XP, and an unfinished challenge returns the card. Owned cards work offline, and an unavailable image ad does not remove the multiplier. Tapping an ad during the timer may leave the app and interrupt the challenge. This feature is not offered in the mainland China App Store region.

Reminders and language

Light haptics for success are on by default, and the short completion tone is off. Changes to these switches are saved immediately. Your reminder can contain up to 16 visible characters and is saved when editing finishes. A custom reminder stays the same when you change languages. Change the app’s language in its iPhone settings.
