Help

Choose a little time to put your phone aside.

Getting started

Choose how long you want to stay off your phone, tap Start phone-free time, then put your phone aside.

During the timer

Keep the app open; the screen will dim automatically. Switching apps, locking the screen or interrupting the timer marks this challenge unfinished. Your previous records remain.

Ending early

Hold End early continuously for 3 seconds to end this challenge. A countdown appears while you hold; release to cancel.

Optional ads

To boost this challenge’s XP, select the ad option before starting. Finish watching and close the ad; the timer starts when you return. No ad click is needed. Closing early or an unavailable ad starts with normal XP. Tapping an ad during the timer may leave the app and interrupt the challenge.

Reminders and language

Edit the timer’s reminder in the app’s settings. Change the app’s language in its iPhone settings.
