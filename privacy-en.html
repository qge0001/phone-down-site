Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Phone Down handles your personal information.

Your information

Your usage records, settings and reminder text are stored on your device. These contents are not sent to the developer or advertising services.

Advertising services

Where XP Boost Cards are available, choosing advertising features may preload or display ads. Google AdMob and its consent service may process your IP address, device-related identifiers, ad views and interactions, and performance and crash information for advertising, analytics, diagnostics and fraud prevention. IP addresses may also be used to estimate approximate location. Google’s privacy policy governs how this information is handled, retained and deleted. XP Boost Cards and ads are not offered in the mainland China App Store region.

System tracking permission

When you actively claim an XP Boost Card, the app may ask for system tracking permission. Only with your permission can Google use your device’s advertising identifier with data from other companies’ apps or websites for personalized advertising and ad measurement. If you decline or tracking is restricted by the system, ad requests do not provide the advertising identifier and ad personalization is turned off. You can still watch ads to earn cards and use challenges. You can change tracking permission in iPhone Settings at any time.

Privacy choices

You choose whether to earn or use XP Boost Cards. Where advertising consent is required, a prompt is shown before an ad is displayed. When needed, use the ad privacy option in the app’s settings to adjust advertising choices. System tracking permission and advertising consent are managed separately; declining system tracking does not reduce card rewards.

Retention and deletion

Records on your device are kept until you delete the app. Deleting the app removes those records from the device; copies in system backups are managed through your backup settings. The developer cannot restore deleted records.

Website and feedback

When you visit this website, GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security. This website adds no analytics or advertising. Feedback submitted through GitHub is handled by GitHub; do not include private information in public reports. External websites have their own privacy policies.

Policy updates and privacy questions

We will update this policy and its date when our information handling changes. For privacy questions, visit the Contact page for support.
